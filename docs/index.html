The problem

  • The form collects contact details and interest, but not what a quote depends on: home ownership, utility, bill or roof.
  • The form's own text says a proposal may be delayed if the utility bill isn't provided, yet the form never asks for it.
  • Phone, email and address are free text, so the same person can come in twice and look like two leads.
  • Referrals are matched on a typed name only, which is the weakest possible key.
  • The appointment time ("11AM") is stored without a time zone. "None of these dates" leaves the record with no date and no status.

Recommendation

  1. Capture automatically first. Add a stable submission ID, a UTC timestamp, campaign source and form version. This needs no extra questions for the visitor.
  2. Clean on arrival. Normalize phone, email and address, then flag likely duplicates. Link them to the earlier record; never delete.
  3. Ask a few optional questions that drive the quote: homeowner, utility, monthly bill (or a bill upload), roof age, timeline.
  4. Store codes, not labels. Fixed values for dropdowns make search, reporting and AI features reliable.

Why it matters

Automatic fields (submission ID, source, timestamp) and light normalization of phone, email, and address keep records consistent from the start. A few optional questions give us the inputs a quote needs, and storing codes instead of labels keeps the data stable for reporting as the form changes.

About the current form: it runs on Cognito Forms. Fields were recorded by hand from the live page in November 2026. Answer choices for "solar products" and "how did you hear", any consent text, and the submit label were not captured.

What changed

  • cleaned Email lowercased, phone in E.164, "Avenue" became "AVE", city uppercased without the period.
  • linked The email and street+ZIP match the first request (Q-0a1f8f6e79f1), so this one is marked a likely duplicate. The phone is different, which is useful for sales to know, so both records are kept.
  • stable The ID is a hash of the cleaned values and time, so a re-run gives the same ID.

Search What clean data makes possible

  • Find a caller's request by any phone format.
  • See every request from one household, linked by duplicate_of.
  • Filter leads by utility, ZIP, campaign or timeline.

AI Uses, each with a person reviewing

  • Read usage and rate from an uploaded bill.
  • Score and route leads using homeowner, roof, bill and timeline.
  • Draft a first proposal from structured fields.
  • Answer staff questions over past quotes, grounded in stable IDs.

The form captures visitor answers plus hidden source and version fields. A checker normalizes contact details, assigns a stable ID and UTC timestamp, and flags possible duplicates. CRM keeps one record per person and property, linking duplicates instead of deleting them and sending flagged leads for review. Search and AI can then look up requests by phone, address, campaign or utility, and use structured fields to draft proposals or answer questions, always with a person reviewing.

Sample data is fake: 555-01xx phone numbers and example.com emails. Code: scripts/quote_check.py in this repository.
